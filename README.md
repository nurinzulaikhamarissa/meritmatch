# MeritMatch

A personalized program discovery and recommendation system that helps university students find suitable merit programs and track their merit progress in one place.


## Problem statement
University students receive information about merit-eligible programs through WhatsApp groups, social media, faculty announcements and student organization platforms. Because this information is scattered and unfiltered, students find it hard to identify programs that match their location, interests, availability and merit requirements. As a result, many students miss opportunities and struggle to collect the merit points they need.
